    How it works

    The draft

    Each spin lands on one squad from one season: an IPL franchise or a World Cup team, depending on the edition you pick at the top. Pick one player and drop him into a slot he really batted in. Picks are final. You get 3 re-rolls, and a squad with nobody who fits gives you a free spin.

    Modes

    IPL: Easy lets you draft anyone, Authentic allows at most 4 overseas players. World Cup: Authentic spins any squad, Nation sticks to one country's squads.

    IPL season

    Ten teams: yours and nine real IPL squads from random years. Everyone plays 14 league matches, 70 in all. The top four go to Qualifier 1, the Eliminator, Qualifier 2 and the final.

    World Cup

    Ten teams: yours and nine real national squads. Everyone plays everyone once, 45 matches in all. The top four go to the semi-finals (1st v 4th, 2nd v 3rd), then the final.

    Batting

    BAT decides how long a batter lasts, POW how fast he scores. Line-up order matters: openers face the new ball, finishers arrive at the death.

    Bowling

    Your five best BWL ratings bowl four overs each. The top two share the powerplay and the death. Good bowlers take wickets and squeeze the run rate; part-timers get hit.

    Captain and keeper

    The best captain you draft leads, and his hidden captaincy rating steadies the side after a wicket. No keeper means more runs leak in the field.

    Opponents

    Each opponent fields its best XI from that year's squad. Any player you drafted is left out of their side, so nobody plays against himself.

    Perfect run

    IPL: all 14 league matches, Qualifier 1 and the final, 16-0. World Cup: all 9 league matches, the semi-final and the final, 11-0. One defeat ends the perfect run, but the trophy is still there to win.
