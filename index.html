    How it works

    The draft

    Each spin lands on one squad from one T20 World Cup. Pick one player and drop him into a slot he really batted in. Picks are final. You get 3 re-rolls per draft, and a squad with nobody who fits your open slots gives you a free spin.

    BAT · Anchor

    How long he stays in. Only your top six count. A 300 chase dies fast if the top order is gone in the powerplay.

    POW · Power

    Strike rate and six-hitting. Your eight biggest hitters count, wherever they bat, so a slugger at number nine still helps.

    BWL · Bowling

    Your four best bowlers, wherever they bat. They conceded 300, but legends in the dugout lift the side: fewer soft dismissals, more boundaries.

    Golden cards

    The Player of the Tournament from each World Cup has a gold card, from Shahid Afridi in 2007 to Sanju Samson in 2026. Spot one, grab him.

    Captain

    Every captain carries a hidden captaincy rating. Draft as many captains as you like; the one with the best rating leads automatically. When a wicket falls, the side wobbles for a couple of overs, and a great leader keeps that wobble small. No captain at all and collapses come in clusters.

    Keeper

    No gloveman costs you a small penalty in the chase.

    Ratings

    Every card is a player at one tournament, so Kohli 2014 and Kohli 2022 are different cards. Numbers are fan estimates from form and reputation in that event, not official data.
